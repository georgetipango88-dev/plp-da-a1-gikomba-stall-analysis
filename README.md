## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded her daily sales and stock information for two weeks. This analysis examines how her business is performing, whether sales are growing, which days perform best, and which products need restocking. The analysis uses core Python to turn the sales and stock records into practical business insights.

## How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1kiGWH8U9JspLqkMol-pbuwqKoDA9rlUB?usp=sharing)

## Top Three Findings

1. **Sales are growing:** Total sales increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing **10.66% week-on-week growth**.

2. **Weekends perform much better:** Average weekend sales were **KES 4,900**, compared with **KES 2,685** on weekdays. Weekend sales were therefore **82.50% higher**.

3. **Tomatoes and Sukuma need restocking:** Both products are currently at or below their specified reorder levels.
